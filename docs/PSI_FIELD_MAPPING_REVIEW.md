# Curated PSI field interpretation

The executable field dictionary is `domain/psi_table_mappings.yaml`. It is a source construction mapping, not a question/answer dictionary. The original CSV headers and bytes remain unchanged. The `value_cells` and `structured_cells` registry tables link every mapped quantity/description to its original logical CSV record, column, checksum and raw expression.

The nine previously documentary tables contain 678 records: SPICE 469 acquisition records; CFI 87 identified test records; E-FIELD 32 test-day summaries; CLD 32 test-day summaries; BRE 18 day summaries; s-Flame 18 day summaries; SLICE 12 session summaries; FLEX-2 eight configuration summaries; DAFT two sessions. Duplicate SPICE times retain separate source records without asserting distinct physical experiments. ACME ignition counts are session counts. FLEX-2 alternatives never become simultaneous conditions or a Cartesian expansion of invented runs.

CLD's original table note defines MFC entries as maximum controller capacities in slpm on a nitrogen basis. They are not realized fuel flow. Its concentration ranges include changes during tests and may extend below the concentration reached before extinction. BRE's original note says chamber pressures varied but are not identified. s-Flame's notes say oxygen and pressure change with vitiation. These distinctions are carried in field roles and original notes.

All ten prior adapters now expose additional recorded content: Saffire specimen dimensions and ignition settings; BASS-II gas composition and instrument settings; BASS sample/session identifiers; FLEX nitrogen, helium and termination; SAME mass loss and aging; SAME-R inlet velocity, durations and the printed Rt/R0 ratio; Flame Design flow parameters and calculated/reported parameters; CFI-G outcomes, burner temperature and comments. The ratio's physical definition is not asserted from its abbreviation. The FLEX CO heading is retained without silently correcting it to CO2. The `Burning rate; mm` column retains its unresolved dimensional meaning. Non-numeric or ambiguous expressions remain exact categorical fields with `EXACT_CELL_SEMANTICS_UNRESOLVED`; they do not become measurements.

NASA's [Flame Design science requirements document](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/Flame_Design_SRD_20100401.pdf), preserved as `data/raw/hackathon-documentation/Flame_Design_SRD_20100401.pdf`, defines Zst as stoichiometric mixture fraction and Tad as adiabatic flame temperature, and its nomenclature defines X as mole fraction. These are parameter semantics, not evidence of measured flame temperature. The SRD is indexed as original documentary content alongside the tables; proposed objectives remain proposed.

Official PSI metadata for all 24 catalogued investigations is registered at documentary depth. Objectives, hypotheses, approaches and hardware descriptions have distinct graph classes and investigation-level relations. None is inherited as a run condition. Official metadata can disagree with tables: both statements retain their source scope. The new PSI-142 CSV is an eight-row figure/publication index, including mixed predicted/measured descriptions; it is modeled as publication references, not eight observations. PSI-115's report and organization document describe simulation data, and PSI-117's seminar is a presentation, not a newly executed experimental table.

Validation is reproducible with `uv run python scripts/validate_psi_science.py --snapshot data/snapshots/hackathon-20261110-scientific-rc7 --output /tmp/psi-validation.json`. The receipt checks every mapped value against its original cell, all graph evidence references, SHACL, and investigation-to-record-to-field routes. These checks establish fidelity and structure, not independent adjudication of every scientific interpretation.


The subsequent source-column audit also models the printed `PI` labels in BASS/BASS-II as investigator attributions, rather than fuel or measurement values. NASA/TM-20210011385, physical page 111, explicitly says the summary matrices are grouped by principal investigator. These labels do not create reconciled global person identities. FLEX's secondary `FLEX Test #` is a separate original identifier and can be queried without replacing its primary run label. A number followed by a scientific unit is not interpreted as a coincident source identifier.

Three additional original reports were acquired from NASA NTRS, with immutable bytes and acquisition checksums: 20140009169 (shared SPICE/SLICE hardware), 20220005205 (methods and qualified findings for all six ACME investigations), and 20210011385 (144-page BASS-II summary). The explicit curation in `domain/psi_report_assertions.yaml` retains exact source-page fragments, method context, uncertainty language, and investigation scope. The ACME campaign relation is a reported grouping; it does not transfer conditions between experiments. Source review corrected a BRE fragment that initially included the preceding E-FIELD paragraph; the replacement begins inside BRE's own result paragraph. Exact text matching alone was insufficient to validate attribution.

Official investigation metadata additionally contributes 352 publication-reference entries from 23 nonempty source collections. Those are catalogue references, not 352 inspected full publications or findings. The processed team table's different count is not substituted for the original metadata inventory. The PSI-142 eight-row original table is searchable as a publication/figure catalogue, with its original figure identifiers and reference fields. It is never eight experimental observations.

The curated extension `ontology/psi_science.ttl` documents the added source record, session, alternative configuration, field definition, material description, publication reference, objective, hypothesis, method, apparatus, attributed finding and campaign distinctions. Quantities stay attached to their source-scoped records; registry cell locators and checksums remain outside domain RDF. The authoritative column-by-column inventory is produced by `scripts/scientific_inventory.py`, and includes nonempty source counts and queryable value-cell links for every header. Unresolved unit or label semantics are retained explicitly, rather than normalized by guessing.


The ACME abbreviation audit uses Table 1 on physical page 3 of NASA NTRS 20220005205: BRE, CLD/CLD Flame, E-FIELD, s-Flame and Flame Design now resolve through the curated profiles, alongside CFI-G. Full calendar dates are matched only to explicitly recorded year/month/day components, or a complete printed date identifier. Mission-day counters are not decoded. The query date never replaces the original source values.

The shared SPICE/SLICE hardware description on physical page 3 of NTRS 20140009169 now supports an apparatus configuration and three instrument descriptions: digital still camera, analog video camera and thermopile radiometer. `usesApparatus`, `usesInstrument` and `hasInstrument` are evidence-backed navigation paths. These are source-reported configurations and instrument types; no serial physical-device identity or use by every experimental run is asserted. The ontology extension is `psi-curated-v4`, with explicit instrument endpoint/evidence SHACL constraints. Ontology loading preserves existing relation policies and reads declared domains/ranges instead of replacing relation definitions with empty defaults.

## Instrument attribute binding (ontology psi-curated-v5)

The shared SPICE/SLICE description supports eight explicitly approved datatype assertions on its three instrument description nodes: camera category with digital signal format and still acquisition, camera category with analog signal format and video acquisition, and radiometer category with thermopile detector type. Each word is present in that instrument's own exact quotation on physical page 3 of NTRS 20140009169. Values are not combined across the separate descriptions. This does not identify serial hardware or establish use in an individual experimental run. Native RDF retains the approved datatype declarations and SHACL validates optional single string values. HTTP ontology navigation exposes the source-backed attributes alongside the evidence paths.

## Source nomenclature and neutral identifiers (ontology psi-curated-v6)

The explicitly reviewed `ScientificTermDefinition` class and `hasTermDefinition` relation attach printed nomenclature to an original reported field. Physical page 5 of the NASA Flame Design science requirements document defines `Tad` as adiabatic flame temperature and `Zst` as stoichiometric mixture fraction. These definitions supply terminology evidence for the matching curated field properties, including CFI-G fields. They do not establish which computational solver was used in another investigation, transfer experimental conditions, or turn a calculated quantity into an observation. Relation evidence retains both the original field and the printed definition. SHACL checks the new definition entities.

The BASS source header `Test #` establishes a secondary recorded identifier. It does not establish a planned or scheduled test. Its property is now the neutral `source:secondary_test_identifier`; `As Run #`, `Test #`, and `Sample #` remain distinct. Raw headers and numbers are unchanged.

Curated multipart quotations now retain each exact original substring, offset, source checksum, and ordinal in the evidence registry. This permits explicitly reviewed fragments on a single page, separated by unrelated text, without guessing a continuous span. Runtime validation rejects changed text, checksums, missing parts, or document mixing. These locators remain outside RDF.

## Auxiliary original records (ontology psi-curated-v7)

Reviewed extension: `SourceAnnotation`, `DataProductReference`, and `MaintenanceRecord` specialize `SourceRecord`. `hasSourceAnnotation` and `referencesSourceRecord` carry explicit original reference markers, with evidence from both the annotation and its target. No physical-run equivalence is asserted. Source annotations are not automatically table-wide definitions.

The nine previously retained but unmodeled auxiliary rows are now explicitly mapped by `domain/psi_auxiliary_rows.yaml`: PSI-98 rows 4–5 and PSI-99 rows 11–12 identify pre-test flow visualization records; PSI-99 rows 13–16 preserve the four original notes; PSI-26 row 67 preserves a dated cleaning activity. None is an experimental run or a fuel sample. Field interpretations are guarded by exact original cell contents in addition to the manifest checksum. Note 1 is the table-wide definition of the derived oxygen column. Notes 2–4 attach only to the actual source records and columns containing their references. Note 4 reports a regression-rate interval, not an independent flame-spread measurement. Its full original text and units remain queryable without inventing point values or copying the interval into independent run observations.

The additional original NASA presentation NTRS 20180005168 (October 2017, 32 pages) is a separately scoped documentary source. Its physical pages 30–31 clarify the Saffire table headings, but some conditions differ from the PSI archive: examples include sample II-6 flow and some specimen thicknesses. Neither source overwrites the other. The presentation's preliminary status, missing footnote bodies, and internal table/narrative differences prohibit treating it as a silent correction of the original PSI CSV. Exact source versions remain distinct.

The auxiliary identifier property `source:data_reference_identifier` identifies a reported data reference rather than a camera device. `source:regression_rate` is a velocity quantity attached to the original annotation, not duplicated into separate measured run values. Its reviewed literal expression must occur unchanged in the original cell; original text and unit remain registered, and interval endpoints normalize to m/s. No midpoint, uncertainty distribution, or causal interpretation is generated. Original column/reference context accompanies the annotation into conversational evidence assembly.
